short-desc: Fuzzing or Fuzz Testing is a testing method that injects invalid, malformed or unexpected inputs into a system to gain information or reveal defects and vulnerabilities. Fuzzing against a specific target would be a concerted effort to find a vulnerability on an asset known to the adversary.
