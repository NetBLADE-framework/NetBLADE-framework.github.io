title: Gift Card Cracking
parent: /tactics/42-payment-card-enumeration
ref-id: TEQ-114
short-desc: The adversary attempts to identify valid gift card details by guessing different values. In some cases the adversary will guess the entire set of data, and in others will have some part of the data (such as the first few digits) and will try to guess the missing details. Adversaries often employ a "brute-force" methodology when guessing, trying all possible variations and/or lists of the most common passwords for example.
