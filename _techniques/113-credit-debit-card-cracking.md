ref-id: TEQ-113
short-desc: The adversary attempts to identify valid payment card details by guessing different values. In some cases the adversary will guess the entire set of data, and in others will have some part of the data (such as BINs) and will try to guess the mising details (such as the CVV). Adversaries often employ a "brute-force" methodology when guessing.
