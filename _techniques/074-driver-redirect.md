title: Driver Redirect
parent: /tactics/23-delivery-redirect
ref-id: TEQ-074
short-desc: Driver Redirect is used once an order has been placed and shipped for delivery. The adversary will contact the delivery organisation and redirect the delivery to a different location such as an alternative address, PO Box, or Amazon secure locker. Alternatively they may use a reshipping company to redeliver the item to a new address.
